# Frozen Work Specification v1

Session: `actual-adaptive-stone-lantern-v2`

## Goal and authority
Create the stone lantern shown in the supplied reference as an inspectable GLB. Its overall silhouette and central square opening must be clearly recognizable. The original request and bound current-reference image are Goal authority. Execution guidance supplies methods, not replacement acceptance criteria.

## Reconstruction
Build the broad low roof, projecting eaves, raised central cap, rectangular lantern chamber, supporting platform, and squat curved lower supports with arch-shaped gaps. Match visible proportions before adding restrained stone weathering. Use coherent modeling units because physical dimensions are unspecified.

Construct the central square aperture as an actual opening through the chamber wall into an interior cavity. Keep its four edges readable and its interior free of opaque geometry that closes the opening. Reconstruct adjacent visible openings consistently. Restore structure hidden by the foreground pole and person, excluding both from the asset. Hidden sides and the precise support arrangement may use conservative approximate symmetry. Avoid unsupported decorative additions.

## Execution strategy
Reuse `blender-modeling-workflow:v1` through the installed headless Blender boundary. Author mesh geometry, export GLB, and retain a supporting blend file if useful. Comfy API readiness is contextual evidence only; generation and model availability are not established and are unnecessary for this strategy.

## Independent evidence and acceptance
Fresh-import the exported GLB into an independent scene. Acquire neutral-material front, back, side, and elevated views at the supported 512×512 resolution. Acquire a material perspective and a square-aperture detail view at the supported 768×768 resolution. Adjust diagnostic framing or request additional supported views when occlusion or depth remains ambiguous.

Inspect the aperture detail together with an oblique or elevated view to distinguish a geometric opening and cavity from a surface patch or recess. A dark region alone is insufficient evidence. Use the available fresh-import bounds, mesh/polygon counts, and object-name report to assess artifact integrity; these reports supply no automatic aperture-topology verdict. Do not require unsupported numerical topology or raycast measurements.

All five mandatory criteria in FinalizedFields must pass on the delivered GLB. Rendered evidence must come from that fresh-imported artifact. If evidence is inconclusive, obtain additional supported diagnostics or revise within the remaining budget; do not weaken the criterion.

## Adaptive resource envelope
Allow up to 3 Production Runs, 9 total Attempts, 9 Worker calls, 12 Reviewer calls, 16 Frontier calls, and 18 independent diagnostic calls across the Session. One Worker call comprises logical code authoring plus a Blender build. Independent fresh-import diagnostic operations consume diagnostic calls.

Each local artifact correction creates a new Attempt under its Workflow. Frontier may version the Workflow strategy and explicitly restart a new Production Run under this same frozen specification. These limits permit ordinary corrections, multiple build/review cycles, meaningful strategy revision and restart, additional evidence, and final acceptance. A semantic REVISE is not terminal by itself. Stop without acceptance if mandatory criteria remain unmet when the relevant budget is exhausted. No wall-clock deadline is imposed.
