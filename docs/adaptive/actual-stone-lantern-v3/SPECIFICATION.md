# Frozen Work Specification v1

Session: `actual-adaptive-stone-lantern-v3`. Request type: `NEW_WORK`. Deliverable: one final inspectable `.glb` stone lantern.

## Goal and authority

Reconstruct the lantern in the supplied `current-reference` image, with its silhouette and central square opening clearly recognizable. The original Korean request and the supplied image establish the subject and acceptance target. Community guidance and runtime observations inform execution only.

## Reconstruction

Model the broad flared roof, low stacked cap, framed chamber, projecting platform and substantial curved support base. Match their visible relative proportions. Construct the square opening through the chamber wall into a hollow interior using actual mesh geometry. Preserve a readable stone frame around it. Complete occluded and hidden regions conservatively; a coherent four-support base is a permissible inference. Do not reproduce the person, foreground stake or background scene.

Use approximately 2 Blender units overall height as an arbitrary working scale. Apply restrained weathered gray-stone materials. Prioritize structure, silhouette and aperture before optional surface detail.

## Execution strategy

Reuse `blender-modeling-workflow:v1` and the installed reviewed headless Blender geometry boundary. Author meshes with bpy, export GLB, and retain a `.blend` as an optional supporting artifact. Comfy endpoint readiness is contextual evidence only; this strategy does not require model acquisition or Comfy generation.

## Acceptance and independent evidence

All four criteria in `finalized_fields_json` are mandatory. Independently import each reviewed candidate GLB into a fresh Blender scene. Acquire canonical front, back, side and elevated views at 512×512 with neutral materials. Acquire a material perspective view and front/oblique aperture-detail evidence at 768×768. Adjust detail camera placement when needed to show the wall opening, frame and hollow interior. Review these images against the supplied reference, accounting for its occlusion. Darkness alone does not prove an aperture.

Record the imported object names, mesh/polygon counts and bounds. This report verifies import and geometry presence; it does not automatically establish aperture topology. Aperture acceptance requires clear independent visual evidence. If the installed evidence cannot resolve the aperture, request additional supported views and retain REVISE or uncertainty until resolved.

## Adaptive execution and limits

The positive limits are totals across this Session: 3 Production Runs, 12 Attempts, 12 Worker calls, 15 Reviewer calls, 18 Frontier calls and 24 diagnostic calls. One Worker call means one logical code-authoring plus Blender build operation. Independent fresh-import diagnostic operations consume diagnostic calls.

Begin with a Workflow and build/review cycle. Local artifact corrections create new Attempts under that Workflow. A semantic REVISE does not terminate the Session. Frontier may revise the Workflow strategy and explicitly start a new Production Run within the frozen goal and criteria. Reserve capacity for at least two initial build/review cycles, a meaningful Workflow revision, an explicit restart, independent evidence and final acceptance. Do not impose a one-Run/one-Attempt restriction or a seed-plus-one correction rule.

Accept and deliver only a specific final GLB whose independent evidence satisfies every mandatory criterion. If limits are exhausted first, report the unmet criteria and preserve the attempt evidence without claiming acceptance. No wall-clock deadline is imposed.
